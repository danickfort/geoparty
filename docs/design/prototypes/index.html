Atlas, explored

Ocean-tinted paper, navy ink, one red for the current stop, the pin and anything urgent. Barlow for text, Barlow Condensed for headings and every number. This page keeps the palette fixed and varies everything else: three ways to lay out the host console, four ways to play a round on the phone, and the big screen that ties them together.

Variants tagged Changes the game alter rules or flow and would need server work. Variants tagged New surface add something players or hosts don't have today but leave the rules alone. Untagged variants are pure redesigns of what exists.

Admin variants

All three assume the same job: get a round ready, run it, and always know where the game is. They differ in where the host stands: at a laptop, at a laptop with a projector preview, or walking around the room with a phone.

A1: Route console

Phases as a route down the left rail, main action right under it, tools beneath. In the lobby the roster is a third column; once the round starts the roster disappears and every player moves into the Guesses list, a dash where there is no guess yet.

A2: Director's desk Changes the game

For a host at a laptop next to the projector. The route runs horizontally in a top bar with the main action beside it. The left half shows what the room sees right now; the right half is a playlist of rounds for the whole evening, loaded from the library before people arrive. "New game" becomes "Next round" and loads the next entry.

The Autopilot switch is the second change: when on, the game advances by itself. The reveal starts a few seconds after time is up, pins drop on a timer, the scoreboard shows for ten seconds, then the next round loads. The host can play too.

A3: Pocket host

The console on a phone, for a host who wants to walk around with a drink. Same route, now as stops on a line; the action sits at thumb height; the tab bar holds Library, Find, Players and History. Round setup collapses to a card that reports its status and opens the full form as a sheet. The laptop console stays for the heavy work; this is the remote for the evening itself.

Where I'd start

Admin: ship A1 as the laptop console and A3 as the same components in a phone layout; they share the route, the action and the guesses list. Treat A2's playlist and Autopilot as the next feature after the redesign, since they change the server's one-round model.

Players: P2 and P4 are free wins that only touch the client. P1 and P5 are small and worth doing; both only surface data the client already has. P3 is the real gameplay experiment: fun to test at one party before committing.
